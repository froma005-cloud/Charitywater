Frequently asked questions

Where does my money go?

Public donations fund water projects. A separate group of private donors covers operations, so 100% of your gift goes to the field.

Will I see the impact?

Yes. We share project updates, photos, and locations so you can follow along and share with friends.

Can I give monthly?

Absolutely. Monthly giving helps us plan with local partners and sustain repairs.
